Get started How it works

Have an invite code? That's all you need. Already in? Go to anywr.me/yourname.

🔐

Logged in, and it stays that way

Sign in to your portals, inboxes and dashboards once. Your profile lives on a fixed IP and is kept between sessions, so there are far fewer "new device" prompts.

🤖

Built for agents

Every browser action is an MCP tool: open, read, click, fill, screenshot. Paste one link into Claude and it can use your browser, and only yours.

🙋

You step in when it counts

2FA prompt or captcha? The agent hands you a live view of the same browser. Click through it and the agent picks up where it stopped.

Three steps

1

Create an account

Pick a username with your invite code and confirm your email with a one-time code. No password. Your browser lives at anywr.me/you.

2

Sign in to your sites

Open the live viewer and log in to what you use, just like on a new laptop. Tick "remember this device" when asked.

3

Hand it to your agent

Create your agent link and add it to Claude as a custom connector (or claude mcp add). Then ask it to "check my grades" or "download last month's invoice".

Get started

One browser per person

Each account gets its own container, network and profile. No other account, and no other agent link, can reach it.

No passwords anywhere

You sign in to anywr with a code sent to your email. Your site logins are typed into the browser by you, never stored by anywr, and Chrome's password manager is off.

Revoke or delete any time

Replacing the agent link kills the old one immediately. Deleting your account wipes the browser profile with it.
